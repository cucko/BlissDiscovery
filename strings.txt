PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY
	EN	Bliss Discovery

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_DESC
	EN	Adds tiles to My Music showing random songs from different genres. Clicking a tile starts a Bliss mix seeded from that song.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_NUM_TILES
	EN	Number of tiles

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_NUM_TILES_DESC
	EN	How many song tiles to show on the Material Skin home screen (1-12). Each tile uses a song from a different genre. Enable the section in Material Skin: Settings > Interface > Home screen items > Bliss Discovery.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_MIX_COUNT
	EN	Mix length

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_MIX_COUNT_DESC
	EN	Number of tracks the Bliss mix should contain (1-50).

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_DSTM
	EN	Enable Don't Stop The Music

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_DSTM_DESC
	EN	When a mix is started, also enable DSTM with Bliss Mixer on that player so the mix keeps going.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REFRESH_AFTER_PLAY
	EN	Replace tile after playing

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REFRESH_AFTER_PLAY_DESC
	EN	After a tile is clicked, replace it with a new song from a genre not currently shown.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REFRESH_HOURS
	EN	Refresh all tiles every (hours)

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REFRESH_HOURS_DESC
	EN	Pick a fresh set of songs periodically. 0 disables. Tiles are also refreshed after every library rescan.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REFRESH_NOW
	EN	Refresh tiles now

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REFRESH_NOW_DESC
	EN	Immediately pick a new set of songs for all tiles.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REGENERATE
	EN	Regenerate

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_MIX_TITLE
	EN	Bliss mix

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_STARTED
	EN	Bliss mix started from "%s" by %s

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_NEW_SONG
	EN	Pick a different song for this tile

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_REPLACED
	EN	Tile replaced. Reload the page to see the new artwork.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_NO_TILE
	EN	This tile is no longer available - the home screen will refresh.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_NO_PLAYER
	EN	No player selected.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_HOME_TITLE
	EN	Bliss Discovery

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_HOME_SUBTITLE
	EN	Tap a song to start a Bliss mix

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_NO_BLISS
	EN	Bliss Mixer plugin not available

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_NO_MIX
	EN	Bliss could not create a mix from "%s" - has this track been analysed?

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_LIBRARY
	EN	Restrict to library

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_LIBRARY_DESC
	EN	Pick tile songs and mix tracks only from this virtual library. "Library selected in Material Skin" follows the library chosen with Material Skin's "Change Library" button, so each browser gets tiles for the library it is browsing. "Player's library view" uses the library view set for the player that is selected in Material Skin (LMS Settings > Player > Library View), so each player can get its own tiles.

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_LIBRARY_ALL
	EN	All music (no restriction)

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_LIBRARY_MATERIAL
	EN	Library selected in Material Skin

PLUGIN_BLISSDISCOVERY_LIBRARY_PLAYER
	EN	Player's library view
